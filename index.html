About This Project

Cinema as a Lens for International Relations Theory

This project explores how popular films and television reflect — and challenge — the foundational concepts of world politics. Each station pairs a major film with a core IR theory: sovereignty, diplomatic negotiation, gender & security, and forced migration.

Rather than treating these films as mere metaphors, we read them as political texts. The train format mirrors the journey of ideas across borders, time periods, and disciplines.
